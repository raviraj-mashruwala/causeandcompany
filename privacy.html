Privacy policy

Cause & Company Studio · last updated 20 November 2026

What this app is

Cause & Company Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @causeandcompany to upload that channel's own videos and manage its own channel settings, playlists and comments. It is not offered to the public.

Data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails, add videos to playlists, post comments and update channel branding on the owner's channel. It reads only what it needs to do this, such as the channel's own playlists and settings.

Where data is kept

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube API.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
